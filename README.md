## 🚀 What I'm Building

### Amigosss

**Amigosss** is an AI-integrated career ecosystem focused on helping
students and professionals bridge the gap between **learning and earning**.

The idea started from a simple observation:

> Skills are valuable, but people also need a way to prove those skills
> and connect them with real opportunities.

I'm working on the product, technology, and overall ecosystem while
continuously learning from the process of building it.
